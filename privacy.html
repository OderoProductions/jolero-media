Privacy

What we do with your information.

Short version: we collect what you type into the enquiry form, we use it to reply to you, and that's it. No cookies, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising, and we never sell anything to anyone.

Last updated 28 July 2026

Who we are

Jolero Media is a sports media agency based in West Sussex, run by Christopher and Justin. For anything on this page — including asking us to delete your details — email info@joleromedia.com.

What we collect, and when

When you fill in the enquiry form. The form asks for your name, email address, Instagram handle, sport, level, club or team, what you're trying to achieve, what you're looking for, your budget range, when you want to start, and anything else you want to tell us. Only the message and club fields are optional — but you decide what to put in every one of them. Don't send us anything sensitive through it.

When you switch between light and dark mode. Your choice is saved on your own device so the site remembers it next time. It never reaches us, and it isn't a cookie — nothing is stored at all unless you actually press the button.

When you simply read the site. Nothing. We don't run analytics and we don't know you were here. Our host keeps standard server logs, the same as every website on the internet, which include IP addresses and are used to keep the service running and secure.

Cookies

This site sets none. That's why there's no annoying banner. If we ever add visitor statistics we intend to use a cookie-free tool, and we'll say so here before we turn it on.

Why we're allowed to hold it

You sent us an enquiry and you want an answer, so we use your details to give you one and to quote for the work. In data-protection terms that's taking steps you asked for before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in running the business. If we later want to email you anything you didn't ask for, we'll ask permission first.

Who else can see it

As few companies as we can manage, and only ones doing a specific job for us:

  • Formspree — delivers the enquiry form to our inbox. Based in the United States.
  • Google Workspace — our email. Your enquiry lands in an inbox there and stays there.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website and keeps the server logs described above.

Nobody else. We don't sell or share your details, we don't run ads, and we don't hand anything to data brokers.

How long we keep it

Enquiries stay in our inbox while there's a realistic chance of working together, and client records for as long as we're working together plus the period we're legally required to keep business and tax records. Ask us to delete you and we will, unless we're legally required to keep something.

The client portal

The portal on this site is currently a demonstration. It runs entirely in your own browser using made-up example data, and nothing in it is sent to us or stored on a server. When we switch it on for real clients we'll update this page first, and explain exactly what gets stored and where.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of what we hold on you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we're using it. Email info@joleromedia.com and we'll sort it — there's no form to fill in and no charge.

If you think we've handled your information badly, please tell us first so we can fix it. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle your information, we'll change this page and update the date at the top. If the change is a significant one and we hold your details, we'll tell you directly.
