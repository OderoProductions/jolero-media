Keeping it clean

A few ground rules.

We did some digging and found a few things worth flagging, since nicotine products come with strict ad rules. FEDRS should double-check the legal side with their own advisers.

  • No claims about what it does. Nothing about calm, focus, energy or performance. It's shown, not sold.
  • UK rules change in June 2027. From 1 June 2027 the UK bans nicotine ads and sponsored posts, so the trial (and any extension) wraps up before then.
  • Poland's already strict. Polish law bans nicotine pouch ads, so the content shouldn't be aimed at a Polish audience.
  • Always labelled. Every reel goes out with the paid partnership tag, and FEDRS signs each one off before it posts.
