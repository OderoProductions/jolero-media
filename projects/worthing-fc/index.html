Featured project · Football

Worthing FC.

A last-minute call, a new signing, and finished films back with the club about three hours after we stopped filming.

The brief

Worthing FC called us in at short notice to announce a new signing, Dominic. They needed his arrival captured properly (the interview, the shirt, the first steps around the club) and ready to post while the news was still news.

What we made

We headed straight to the stadium and filmed a short interview with Dominic, plus B-roll of him pulling on the shirt for the first time and being introduced to the club.

From that one visit the club got two separate edits: a punchy signing announcement and a standalone interview. We then put together this director's cut, bringing the two together with B-roll laid over the interview, so the whole story lands in under a minute.

The result

Both edits were with the club roughly three hours after we wrapped, and that includes getting back, offloading the footage and cutting it. Short notice in, same-day announcement out.
