Coach ManoWar.

Raw, authentic coaching session content for Coach Andre: the work on the gym floor, as it really happens.

01 · PT sessions

Coaching, unfiltered

Andre is a personal trainer and coach who works with fighters and everyday clients alike. He didn't want polished adverts. He wanted real sessions, with real effort, that show what training with him is actually like.

The brief

Raw, authentic clips of Andre coaching: his cues, his energy and his clients pushing through the work, cut for Instagram and TikTok.

What we made

We filmed his sessions with as little interruption as possible and cut two vertical edits with captions: a one to one session with Kieran and a group session. Alongside them we delivered a full set of photos from the gym, in a private gallery.
