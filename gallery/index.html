Need a change?

  1. Click the video to open it. It fills the screen, with the comments beside it.
  2. Play to the moment you want to talk about and pause there.
  3. Type what you'd like changed. The time is attached for you, so we know exactly where you mean — untick it if your note is about the whole edit.
  4. Add your name and press Comment. Comments already left show as marks under the video; click one to jump straight to that moment.

Each comment comes straight to us by email, and the thread stays on the video so you can see what's already been sent. Send as many as you like — it's easier for us in one round than a few at a time. To keep a copy, use the green Download button at the top of that screen.
